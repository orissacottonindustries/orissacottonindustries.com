About Us

Built around practical recycling and responsible growth.

Orissa Cotton Industries Pvt. Ltd. is a manufacturing company focused on recycling and processing cotton waste into useful, high-quality cotton products.

We source cotton waste from local tailors, garment industries, textile units and other sources, then process it into recycled coloured cotton suitable for mattresses, cushions, pillows, upholstery and additional textile applications.

Our long-term aim is to promote sustainability, create employment opportunities and expand our business across India and international markets.

Our Purpose

Less waste. More value. More opportunity.
